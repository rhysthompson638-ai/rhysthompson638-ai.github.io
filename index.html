Good to know

Questions people might ask.

Is FlowPilot AI a fully automated AI platform already?

No. This website is an early-stage prototype. The project planner uses transparent built-in pricing rules. A real AI-powered builder and automatic deployment system would be developed and tested in later stages.

Does the project planner send or save my answers?

No. This demo calculates the estimate in your browser. It does not upload the information to a server or store it in a database.

Will everything be free?

The starter website and demo can be hosted using free services, subject to their terms and limits. Customer-specific hosting, domains, AI usage, email, SMS and integrations may cost money. These must be agreed before work starts.

Can you build for any type of business?

We can assess requests from many industries. We only accept work we can deliver safely and competently, and we may decline regulated, high-risk or unsupported requirements.

How does the half-price offer work?

The offer is intended for the first five eligible customers and applies to the agreed first build labour price only. Ongoing and third-party costs are not discounted unless expressly agreed in writing.
